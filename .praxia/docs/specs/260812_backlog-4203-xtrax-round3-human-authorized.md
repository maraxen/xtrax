## Amendment 1 (D1, blocking) -- supersedes corr-3.md's C1 FIX text

**Original text (corr-3.md, superseded):** "add `higher_is_better: Mapping[str, bool]` as a new REQUIRED (no default) keyword parameter on BOTH `run_multi_iteration_loop` and `run_campaign_loop`, threaded flat and forwarded UNCHANGED on every iteration -- joining the 'static per-campaign, forwarded unchanged' bucket ... explicitly NOT the loop-local-mutated bucket (best_fitness)."

**Amended text:** `higher_is_better: Mapping[str, bool]` remains a new REQUIRED (no default) keyword parameter on BOTH `run_multi_iteration_loop` and `run_campaign_loop` -- its origin, requiredness, and the fact that its OWN VALUE never changes across a campaign are all unchanged from corr-3.md. What changes: `run_multi_iteration_loop`'s per-iteration call to `run_one_candidate_pass` passes `higher_is_better=None if best_fitness is None else higher_is_better` (the loop's own received parameter) -- NOT the raw received value unconditionally. This makes `higher_is_better`'s FORWARDING conditional on `best_fitness`'s own None-ness (matching it call-by-call), while its STORAGE remains the simple "received once, never mutated" pattern C1-Alt-C was rejected in favor of preserving (C1-Alt-C proposed folding `higher_is_better` into `best_fitness`'s own mutable STATE slot, which is a different and still-correctly-rejected idea from conditionally suppressing what's PASSED on a given call).

## Amendment 2 (D3, warning) -- supersedes corr-3.md's AC bullet [C2]

**Original AC bullet [C2] (corr-3.md, superseded):** "...an integration test spies on `run_multi_iteration_loop`'s own call to `run_one_candidate_pass` (`multi_iteration_loop.py:390-408`) AND `run_campaign_loop`'s own call to `run_multi_iteration_loop` (`loop_run.py:507-532`), asserting all 9 values (`frozen_context`, `current_config`, `repo`, `ratchet_ref_name`, `commit_tree_sha`, `callable_name`, `concrete_inputs`, `higher_is_better`, `candidate_target_path`) are forwarded unchanged at both layer boundaries across >=2 iterations."

**Amended AC bullet [C2]:** "...an integration test spies on `run_multi_iteration_loop`'s own call to `run_one_candidate_pass` (`multi_iteration_loop.py:390-408`) AND `run_campaign_loop`'s own call to `run_multi_iteration_loop` (`loop_run.py:507-532`), asserting all 10 values (`frozen_context`, `current_config`, `repo`, `ratchet_ref_name`, `commit_tree_sha`, `callable_name`, `concrete_inputs`, `higher_is_better`, `candidate_target_path`, `allow_fresh_start_despite_existing_lineage`) are forwarded unchanged at both layer boundaries across >=2 iterations -- with the caveat that `higher_is_better`'s forwarding assertion at the `run_one_candidate_pass` boundary specifically must account for Amendment 1's conditional-None-suppression rule (expect `None` on the call where the spy observes `best_fitness=None`, the real mapping on every other call), not byte-identical forwarding on every single call the way the other 9 values are."

## Amendment 3 (mechanical consequence of Amendment 1) -- new AC bullet

**New AC bullet [R3-D1]** (renamed from a round-3-draft `[D1]` to avoid colliding with round 2's own objection ID `D1` -- flagged as R3-C4 by round-3's own verification pass): A test with `best_fitness=None` on the FIRST call of a campaign asserts `run_one_candidate_pass` is called with `higher_is_better=None` regardless of what real mapping `run_multi_iteration_loop` itself received -- verifying the ValueError guard at `main_loop.py:461-467` is never tripped. A second test asserts the SAME suppression holds on ANY subsequent call where `best_fitness` is STILL `None` -- not just "iteration 2", but genuinely N>1 calls in, covering the case where multiple early candidates fail dispatch/hard-blocked gates before any candidate is ever accepted (per `OneCandidatePassResult.accepted`, `main_loop.py:202-226`, which depends on `run_result.success`/`gate_outcome.hard_blocked` in addition to the ratchet sentinel, so `best_fitness` can legitimately remain `None` across several consecutive calls, not just call 1) -- the suppression logic is keyed on `best_fitness`'s live state, not a fixed iteration index, and the test must actually exercise that, not just the single-candidate-then-accept happy path. A third test asserts `higher_is_better` is forwarded as the real, original mapping once `best_fitness` becomes non-`None` (post-accept) -- verifying the suppression lifts correctly and isn't a permanent downgrade to `None` for the rest of the campaign.

## Amendment 4 (R3-C1, blocking -- found by round-3's own verification pass) -- supersedes corr-3.md's AC bullet [C1]

Amendment 2 fixed `[C2]`'s forwarding-spy claim but missed that `[C1]` itself makes the identical now-false claim ("a spy on `run_one_candidate_pass`'s consecutive calls shows `higher_is_better` forwarded byte-identical on every iteration" -- `corr-3.md:103`), directly contradicted by Amendment 1's conditional suppression. This document's line 36 (below, now corrected) previously claimed `[C1]` was "carried forward verbatim... unchanged," which was itself wrong -- the same class of drift D3/R3-C1 already caught elsewhere.

**Amended AC bullet [C1]:** Calling `run_multi_iteration_loop` or `run_campaign_loop` without `higher_is_better` raises `TypeError` (no default) before any candidate is dispatched. A spy on `run_one_candidate_pass`'s consecutive calls shows `higher_is_better` forwarded per Amendment 1's conditional rule -- `None` on every call where the same spy observes `best_fitness=None`, the real, unmutated mapping `run_multi_iteration_loop` received at loop start on every other call -- NOT byte-identical unconditional forwarding the way the other 8 static kwargs (`frozen_context`, `current_config`, `repo`, `ratchet_ref_name`, `commit_tree_sha`, `callable_name`, `concrete_inputs`, `candidate_target_path`) are forwarded.
